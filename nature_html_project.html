Why Nature Matters

Spending time outdoors can help us appreciate the environment and understand why protecting natural habitats is important. Small choices, such as reducing waste and respecting wildlife, can make a difference.

Three ways to enjoy nature responsibly

  • Stay on marked trails to protect plants and soil.
  • Take reusable water bottles and bring your rubbish home.
  • Observe wildlife from a safe distance and never feed wild animals.
